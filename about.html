About SG Taekwon-Do

Built On Strong Foundations

Traditional ITF Taekwon-Do, modern coaching and a club culture that values every student's journey.

Who We Are

SG Taekwon-Do is an ITF Taekwon-Do club serving the local community. We welcome students from young children through to adults, with training designed to develop both martial-arts ability and personal confidence.

Our club culture is centred on the core values of respect, courtesy, integrity, perseverance and self-control.

The goal is not simply to become a better martial artist — it is to become a better version of yourself.

What Students Develop

Training is structured to support physical, technical and personal development.

Confidence

Build confidence through achievable goals, technical progression and supportive coaching.

Discipline

Learn to train with focus, consistency and respect for instructors and fellow students.

Resilience

Learn how effort, perseverance and preparation contribute to long-term progress.
