Where It Began

A Club With A Purpose

SG Taekwon-Do was founded in Campbeltown in 2021 by former World and European Champion Sean Grady, IV Degree. Drawing on his experience in competitive Taekwon-Do, Sean established the club to share his knowledge of the martial art and create opportunities for people in the local community to learn, develop and achieve.

The vision was about more than producing successful competitors. SG Taekwon-Do was formed to give children, young people and adults a welcoming place to build confidence, improve fitness, learn practical martial-arts skills and develop qualities that matter both inside and outside the dojang.

From a first class to a major championship, every student should have the chance to set goals, work towards them and take pride in their progress.

Dream. Believe. Prepare. Every achievement starts with the decision to take that first step.
More Than Martial Arts

What We Aim To Develop

Our training supports the whole person. Technical ability matters, but so do confidence, character, health and the friendships built along the way.

Skill & Self-Defence

Learn ITF Taekwon-Do fundamentals, patterns, techniques and controlled sparring through structured training and progressive development.

Fitness & Wellbeing

Improve strength, flexibility, coordination, agility and stamina while enjoying an active pursuit that can support physical and mental wellbeing.

Confidence & Character

Develop self-belief, focus, perseverance and self-control by setting goals, learning from challenges and celebrating progress.

Respect & Community

Train alongside others in a supportive environment where courtesy, integrity, respect and teamwork are part of the journey.

Learning & Achievement

Progress through structured training and grading opportunities, with guidance to help each student work towards their own goals.

Competition & Opportunity

For students who want to test themselves, the club can offer a pathway into competition, squad opportunities, seminars and events.

A Growing Record Of Achievement

From Campbeltown To The Championship Stage

Since its foundation, SG Taekwon-Do has gone on to train Scottish, British, European and World Champions. This achievement reflects the commitment of students, the support of families and the hard work invested in training, preparation and continuous development.

Competition success is something the club is proud of, but it is not the only measure of progress. Every student who grows in confidence, reaches a new grade, improves their fitness or supports a teammate is part of the club's story.

Our ambition is to keep building an environment where people can aim high, support one another and take pride in what they achieve.
