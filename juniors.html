What We Work On

Progressive junior training combining traditional Taekwon-Do skills with confidence, discipline, fitness and teamwork.

  • Patterns and fundamental techniques
  • Controlled sparring and pad work
  • Coordination, balance and agility
  • Confidence and discipline
  • Grading preparation and achievement

Training Focus

Technique

Progress Through Training

GRADING & ACHIEVEMENT

Juniors are encouraged to set goals, work towards their next grading and recognise the progress they make through regular training.

  • Preparation for grading assessments
  • Development of technical knowledge
  • Goals that encourage continued progress
  • Recognition of individual achievement
  • Opportunities to develop confidence and experience
