YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

This notice explains how SG Taekwon-Do handles personal information in connection with club membership and activities.

1. Who we are

SG Taekwon-Do is a Taekwon-Do club based in Campbeltown, Scotland and is the data controller for the personal information covered by this notice. You can contact the club through our Contact Us page.

2. What information we collect

Depending on your relationship with the club, we may collect names, dates of birth or age groups, contact details, parent or guardian details, emergency contacts, attendance and membership information, grading and training records, health information relevant to safe participation, and photo/video permissions. We may also keep records of incidents, injuries, complaints or safeguarding concerns where necessary. Health information is special-category personal data and receives additional protection.

3. Why we use information

We use personal information where necessary to administer membership and classes; contact students, parents or guardians; manage attendance, grading and club activities; support safe participation and respond to medical needs or incidents; record photo/video choices; meet applicable legal, insurance, safeguarding or governing-body requirements; and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary. We do not use health information for unrelated purposes.

4. Lawful grounds for processing

The lawful basis depends on the purpose and may include legitimate interests in running a community sports club safely and effectively, taking steps requested before or during membership, compliance with a legal obligation, or consent where appropriate. Where health information is processed, an additional UK GDPR Article 9 condition must also apply. The club will document the applicable basis and condition. Optional photo/video permissions are recorded separately and can be withdrawn for future use where consent is the basis; withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

5. Children and parents or guardians

The club welcomes children and young people. We aim to explain our use of their information clearly and in an age-appropriate way. We may communicate with a parent or guardian where appropriate for membership, safety and administration, and take reasonable steps to check that an adult providing information or permission on behalf of a child is authorised to do so.

Photography and video

Photographs and videos may be taken during club training, gradings, competitions and events for club-related communications and promotion, including the club website and official social media. The club uses its Photo and Video Permission Form to record the uses that students or their parents or guardians permit. Permission is optional and is not a condition of joining or participating in the club. Where permission is required for a particular use, the club will not publish identifiable images or videos for that use unless the required permission has been recorded. If no completed form has been received, permission will be treated as not recorded. Permission may be withdrawn at any time by contacting the club. The club will take reasonable steps to stop future use and, where practicable, remove relevant material from channels under its control. Images will be handled responsibly, with particular care for children, privacy and safeguarding.

Health information and participation

All students, or a parent or guardian where appropriate, are expected to complete a health form before taking part and to tell the club about relevant changes. If no completed health form has been provided, the club will have no health declaration on record and may provisionally proceed on the basis that no health conditions or restrictions have been disclosed. This is not confirmation that a student has no health issues or is medically fit to participate. The club may request a completed form or further information before participation where needed for safety. Health information will be handled confidentially and accessed only by authorised people who need it for legitimate club, safety or safeguarding purposes.

6. Who can access information

Access is limited to authorised people who need information for their club role. Health declarations and incident records are restricted records and are not placed in public website repositories or public links. We may share relevant information with emergency services, healthcare professionals, insurers, governing bodies, professional advisers, regulators or public authorities where necessary and lawful, including for safety, safeguarding or legal purposes.

7. Storage and security

Club records may be stored in access-restricted digital folders, secure club systems or paper records kept securely. We use appropriate measures to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, disclosure or alteration. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely risk-free. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details and contact the club if you need an alternative way to provide information.

8. How long we keep records

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, taking account of safety, membership administration, safeguarding, insurance and legal requirements. Our normal approach is to retain relevant student records while the student is active at the club and then review them for deletion, anonymisation or limited retention for up to five years after the student leaves. This is a normal review limit, not a promise that every record will be retained for the full period. Health declarations and other sensitive records are reviewed separately and may be deleted earlier when no longer needed. A record may be retained longer only where there is a documented, lawful and necessary reason, such as an unresolved incident, safeguarding matter, insurance issue or actual or reasonably anticipated legal claim. Financial or other records may have separate statutory or contractual retention requirements.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, objection or data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. These rights are not absolute and exceptions may apply. Contact the club through our Contact Us page to ask a question or make a request. We may need to verify identity before responding.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns, please contact the club first so we can try to resolve them. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

11. Website and cookies

This notice covers personal information handled for club activities. Website analytics, cookies, embedded third-party content, contact forms and other services may collect information. The club will check the live website configuration and provide any additional cookie or service-specific information required.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our activities, systems or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this website.
