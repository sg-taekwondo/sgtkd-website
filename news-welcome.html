Welcome to SG Taekwon-Do.

Our website is the place to discover more about the club, our training pathways and the people who make SG Taekwon-Do such a positive community.

From our Ninjas and Juniors through to our Adult classes, students can develop their Taekwon-Do skills, build confidence and continue their journey through structured training and progression.

More Than Training

SG Taekwon-Do is about more than what happens inside the dojang. Throughout the year our students take part in gradings, competitions, seminars, events, fundraising and other activities that bring our club community together.
